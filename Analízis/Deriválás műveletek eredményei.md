[[Deriválás]]
[[Gyakori derivált formák]]
$$
(c \times f)' = c \times f'
$$
$$
\left(\frac{f}{c} \right)' = \frac{f'}{c}
$$
$$
(f + g)' = f' + g'
$$
$$
(f \times g)' = f' \times g + f \times g'
$$
$$
\left( \frac{f}{g} \right)' = \frac{f'\times g - f \times g'}{g^{2}}
$$
$$
\left( \frac{c}{f} \right)' = \frac{-c \times f'}{f^{2}}
$$
