Wide area network.

![[Pasted image 20260909110135.png]]

The host and the subnet are usually operated by different people, and there are complications that arise from using really long cables.

Many WANs are internetworks, because the transmission lines may require SONET links.

## VPN-s
![[Pasted image 20260909110812.png]]

VPN-s have Virtualization, meaning they provide a flexible reuse of internet connectivity, but you also have no real control of the resource. It would be very easy to add another office to this, but the quality depends heavily on the signal strength, as it doesn't have cables.

## Network service providers, ISP-s

Another type of WAN, they own the subnet, and charge money for giving people access to it.
